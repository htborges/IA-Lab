# Acme Cloud Support

Support is available 24/7 through chat and email at support@acme-cloud.example.

To reset your password, open the login page, click "Forgot password" and follow the link sent to your email. Reset links expire after 30 minutes.

Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase for the Starter and Pro plans. Enterprise contracts follow the terms in the signed agreement.

Planned maintenance happens on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC. Customers are notified 72 hours in advance.
