# Acme Cloud Plans

Acme Cloud offers three hosting plans: Starter, Pro and Enterprise.

The Starter plan costs 5 USD per month and includes 1 vCPU, 1 GB of RAM and 25 GB of SSD storage. It is ideal for personal websites and small projects.

The Pro plan costs 20 USD per month and includes 4 vCPUs, 8 GB of RAM, 160 GB of SSD storage and daily backups.

The Enterprise plan has custom pricing. It includes dedicated hardware, a 99.99% uptime SLA and a dedicated account manager. Contact sales to get a quote.
